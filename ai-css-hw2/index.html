About

I spend my working life somewhere between a muddy riverbank and a spreadsheet — collecting field observations by day and turning them into models, maps and dashboards by night. Ten years in, I still keep every notebook I've ever filled.

My background sits at the crossing of ecology and data engineering: I design monitoring programs, build the pipelines that clean and store what they collect, and translate the results into something a policy team can actually use. I like problems that start outdoors and end in code.
